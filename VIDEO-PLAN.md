# Lamatna — two-minute demonstration advertisement

Record at 1920×1080 in landscape. Combine a phone recording of students playing with a desktop screen recording. Use clear narration and readable captions; keep music low enough to hear voices. Show actual analysed footage and actual results. Do not manufacture goals, scores, accuracy numbers or training claims.

## Record these first

1. A steady 15–30-second football clip with clear, distinct players, a visible ball and visible goal mouths. Include an actual goal or a clearly missed shot so you can check the result.
2. A short clip for each original game. Keep spoken instructions and gameplay clear; English speech recognition is the current local option.
3. A screen recording of one complete workflow: upload → observations → host review → points → results. Capture the original video and result together so judges can compare them.
4. One brief shot showing the students using and explaining their own app.

Two people are enough: one films and narrates; both take turns demonstrating. The third teammate, if available, can edit/upload. Avoid spending the core demonstration on decorative animations. If a game still has a limitation, name it briefly rather than presenting it as solved.

Upload to YouTube as Unlisted. Test playback signed out. Target two minutes; the problem permits a longer demo if necessary, but keep it concise.
